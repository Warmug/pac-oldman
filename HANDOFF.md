## 1. What this is

A PAC3 outfit for Garry's Mod (SCP-RP server) that turns the playermodel
`models/player/Suits/male_08_shirt_tie.mdl` into a ~140-year-old man. It keeps the model's
own head; there are no custom head models (the `oldman/` folder holds an older, unused attempt).

The outfit is generated by Python (`generator/`). Nothing in it is hand-edited. Every prop is
a procedural OBJ plus textures in `props/`, loaded by pac from
`https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Warmug/pac-oldman/main/props/...`. So **prop files must be
on `main`** before the outfit can load them.

### In game
1. Copy `outfit/old_man_2_male08.txt` to `garrysmod/data/pac3/old_man_2_male08.txt`.
2. Run `pac_wear_parts old_man_2_male08` in the console.

### Toggles (each one: `pac_event <name> 2`)
| name | what |
|---|---|
| `cane` | cane in the right hand (holdtype normal); hooked on the belt with any other holdtype; between the knees when sitting; hidden when crouching |
| `smoke` | cigarette in the left fingers, a drag every 13 s with an exhale puff; in the mouth with other holdtypes or crouching; seated version when sitting |
| `glasses` | round wire spectacles with clear lenses |
| `mask` | clear plastic oxygen mask with a green strap (while it's on, the cigarette is only held, no drags) |
| `tank` | oxygen cylinder on a wheeled cart at his left, tube to the mask |
| `cast` | plaster long-leg cast on the LEFT leg, upper thigh to above the ankle |
| `iv` | IV pole with a saline bag on its own wheeled stand at his right (mirror of the cart), line to a cannula on the back of the right hand |
| `watch` | pocket watch: chain from belt loop to pocket, and every 31 s (when still, standing, not smoking) he pulls it out and checks it |

The posture (hunch, bent knees, hanging arms, head tremor, idle sway) is always on.

## 3. IMPORTANT: data that is NOT on GitHub

`build/male08/` is gitignored, but the whole fit depends on it:
- `male08_dump.txt`: skeleton and mesh (bind pose, weights)
- `male08_dump2.txt`: animation poses and textures (the 2nd export)
- `male08_dump3.txt`: per-pose dump, a fresh clientside model per pose (the one actually used for poses)
- `male08_dump4.txt`: sit poses (`sit_act_head{-30,0,30}`, sit, sit_rollercoaster, sit_zen, sit_* weapon variants, drive_pd, `sit_c*`)
- `seqs.txt`: sequence list
- `tex_*.png`: the model's textures (used only by the previews)

These are in **`male08_data.zip`**, sent alongside this file. Unzip it into `build/` so you get
`build/male08/...`. If it's ever lost, regenerate it in single-player GMod with the
exporters in `tools/gmod/`:
- `dump_male08.lua`
- `dump_male08_poses.lua`
- `dump_male08_anims.lua`
- `dump_male08_sit.lua`

Each writes a `.txt` into `garrysmod/data/`.

`male08.Model` loads `male08_dump.txt`, then auto-loads dump3 and dump4 from the same
folder if present.

## 6. PAC3 facts learned (verified in the pac3 source)

- **bone3**: each part post-multiplies its bone's world matrix by `Translate(pos) * Rotate(ang)` and
  moves all descendants rigidly. Parts on *different* bones commute. Parts on the *same* bone apply
  in the order they were **last shown** (`ent.pac_bone_parts`, appended in `OnShow`), not tree
  order. **Never stack two non-commuting parts on one bone that can toggle independently.** Fold
  the extra rotation into one part, use a separate copy, or keep both on the same axis.
- **Events AND together.** Express OR with duplicate copies. `Invert=true` means "show when the
  condition is true".
- Events used: command (pac_event), holdtype, `is_crouching`, and `is_sitting`
  (true in any vehicle except the prisoner pod, or sit-anywhere's GetSitting).
  See the helpers in `pacgen.py`.
- **Proxies**:
  - Expressions are Lua, compiled with `pac.CompileExpression`. These words are blacklisted:
    `repeat`, `until`, `function`, `end`.
  - `time()` is RealTime. `owner_velocity_length()` gives speed.
  - Prefer numeric proxies (Size, Width, NumberParticles, Angles) over proxying `Hide`.
  - `if_event(name, a, b)` exists, but it finds the event by name and uses an editor-only flag.
    Avoid it.
- **Model Alpha** is applied with `render.SetBlend` and overrides the material `$alpha`, so transparency
  goes on the model part (`Translucent=true`, `Alpha<1`). Size 0 or Alpha 0 means not drawn.
- **AimPart**: world angles = `Angles + (aim - pos):Angle()` per component.
- **Unknown bone `"none"`**: falls back to `ent:GetPos()` with eye yaw (pitch 0). Used for the cart
  and IV stand jiggles so they don't swing with the pelvis.
- **Particles**: `NumberParticles=0` emits nothing. `DoubleSided=true` emitted each puff twice
  (set to false).
- **Beam**: tex coord = segment/TextureStretch. Curve = lerp plus bend terms
  (`sim.beam_points` mirrors it).
- On death pac moves outfits onto the ragdoll.
- **Sit animations** (gmod base gamemode):
  - Chairs use `ACT_HL2MP_SIT` ("sit").
  - Other seats use `sit_rollercoaster` (same pose, 4 units lower).
  - Prisoner pod uses `drive_pd`; jeep `drive_jeep`; airboat `drive_airboat`.
  - With a weapon allowed in the vehicle: `sit_<holdtype>`.
  - The solver uses `sit_act_head0`.

## 7. Feature notes and key numbers

### Posture
- Hunch on spine bones; `neck (0,36,0)`; bent knees on thigh/calf/foot.
- Holdtype-gated so weapon aim isn't wrecked: unarmed = normal/passive/fist. Armed holdtypes get
  a back shape that keeps the aim on target.
- Arm hang parts are solved on the standing idle and hidden while crouching.

### Cane (right hand, `cane_scale 0.93`)
- **Standing**:
  - Solved so the tip is about 6 ahead of and 9 outside the right foot (it holds weight).
  - The cane aims at a far marker on the pelvis (AimPart), so it stays upright while walking.
- **Belt and crouch**: on the belt for other holdtypes; hidden while crouching.
- **Sitting**:
  - The right fist rests on top of the handle; the cane stands between the knees, tip between the feet.
  - Lean ~11° back; elbow hangs ~10 below the shoulder.
  - Grip `cane_hand_sit` = the standing grip rotated −90° about the hand's z axis (the handle axis),
    so the wrist stays within ~31° of the animation.
  - Seated solve cost 1.4.

### Cigarette (left hand)
- 13 s cycle:
  - Blend `s = smoothstep(clamp(min(T, 3.4-T)))*STILL`.
  - Drag holds about 1–2.4 s.
  - Exhale puff 3.45–4.35 s.
- Smoke was reduced at the user's request:
  - tip smoke FireDelay 0.32, alpha 38;
  - mouth puff only after each drag.
- **Standing drag**: elbow out and forward with no chest clipping (cost 6.666; keep this solution).
- **Seated drag**: its own solve on the sit pose. A **bow** term `bow*4s(1-s)` (zero at both ends)
  routes the hand around the cane and right fist. Hands stay ≥0.8 apart.
- **Seated with the cast on**: a separate copy (`cig_arm_sit_cast`) rests the hand on the plaster.
- Crouching, other holdtypes, or sitting with other holdtypes: the cigarette goes in the mouth.

### Oxygen
- Clear mask shell and lenses: Alpha on the model parts (the user once saw them opaque in game,
  which is why).
- Cart sits at `(-7, 23, pelvis_z)` on the `"none"` origin bone with a jiggle (speed 7, strain 0.45).
- Tube is a beam from the mask outlet via a clip on spine 2.

### Leg cast (left leg)
- Built from dense skin samples (28 barycentric points per triangle), per-slab convex hulls over
  14 poses, smoothed.
- Thigh tube on the left thigh bone; shin tube plus a knee "drum" (a surface of revolution about
  the knee hinge, so bending never opens a gap) on the calf.
- Stockinette cuffs at the top and ankle. The user asked to remove the shoe; the plaster ends above the ankle.
- `leg_cast.margin(piece, pts)` gives the exact inside/outside test against the plaster.
- **Sitting**: `cast_lift_sit` adds extra elbow bend (same axis as the hang part, so it commutes)
  and a hand turn, so the left hand rests on top of the plaster.

### IV drip
- Pole with 5-leg casters at `(cart_x, -cart_y, cart_z)`.
- Saline bag labelled "0.9% Sodium Chloride / 1000 mL" with a drip chamber, roller clamp at z 56.
- Line is a beam (width 0.3, bend 7) to the cannula on the back of the right hand.
- No special fluid colour (the user's choice).

### Pocket watch
- Gold, Roman numerals, small seconds dial at VI, "1886".
- Chain beam runs from the belt loop to the pocket.
- Check animation every 31 s, only while standing still, holdtype normal, not crouching, not
  sitting, not smoking:
  - arm offsets fade in;
  - head tips down 20°;
  - the second hand ticks with `floor(time())`.

## 8. Known limits and ideas

- Seated poses only match chairs and normal seats. In a jeep or airboat the seated cane and arm
  look wrong (workaround: `pac_event cane 0`). Not solved: drive_jeep, drive_airboat, drive_pd.
- The watch check is off while sitting.
- The seated solves are for holdtype normal only.
- The cane renders "dashed" in previews (texture sampling in the preview only), not in game.
- Adding features: the user picks ideas one at a time and wants an RP reason for each one.
  Already built that way: the leg cast, the IV, the pocket watch.
